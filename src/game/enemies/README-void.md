# void — Void Swarm Enemy Faction (AF-049, game layer, inside `enemies/`)

**Purpose:** The fourth enemy faction — and the first that is not a civilisation. Where Outlaw squads SCATTER (fear, binary), Machine networks DEGRADE (logic, discrete steps), and the Crystal Ecosystem WEAKENS continuously from a living count, the Void Swarm CORRUPTS: a level that climbs over time for as long as a Void Beacon survives, decays once every Beacon is contained, and steps down immediately on any single Beacon kill. Time itself is part of this doctrine, not just who is still alive.

**Responsibilities:** Unit/combat-style/special-mechanic/network-trait/reality-distortion/mini-boss/elite-gain vocabulary, six full sandbox `EnemyDef`s with real AF-032 `WeaponDef` ranged attacks, visual-language record, and corruption/zone tuning (`voidData`); the time-varying corruption level (hard-capped for fairness), Beacon tracking, and the Corruption Zone factory (`createCorruptionZone`) (`VoidCorruption`).

**Dependencies:** `enemies/enemyData` (`EnemyDef` — unchanged; every def uses the dormant `voidEntity` family, registered since AF-033 and unused until now). `bosses/BossArena` (`HazardZoneDef`/`stepHazardZone`/`isInsideHazard` — Corruption Zones' exact engine, unchanged; the third faction to reuse it, and the first enemy-faction hazard to carry a live status effect via `statusOnTick`, following AF-036's biome-hazard precedent). `enemies/OutlawSquad` (`OutlawSquadRuntime.formationOffsets` — spawn placement, reused a third time). An Ancient Void Avatar IS an AF-034 Elite via the unchanged spawn path. **Deliberately does not reuse AF-039's faction-profile system** — the Void Swarm is explicitly "not a civilisation" per its own spec, so it gets no `FactionDef`, no reputation, no relationships; its Codex entry has no related faction entry to point at, unlike every prior faction module.

**Data structures:** `VoidCorruptionRuntime`, `CorruptionSnapshot`, `VOID_CORRUPTION_TUNING`, `VOID_ZONE_TUNING`.

**Extension points:** new organisms are `EnemyDef` data (seven of thirteen registered kinds await defs); new swarm compositions are id lists; mini-boss kinds bind to AF-035 `BossDef` content when authored; further Void Network traits (`VOID_NETWORK_TRAITS`) and Reality Distortion actions (`REALITY_DISTORTION_ACTIONS`) attach to the same corruption-level/hazard-engine surfaces.

**Known limitations:** of the ten Special Mechanics, Corruption Zones are mechanically live (via the Void Beacon's corruption climb and the hazard engine); Reality Tears, Gravity Wells, Teleportation-as-a-mechanic (distinct from the Shadow Hunter's own movement), Dimensional Gates, Phase Shifting, Dark Energy Pulses, Spatial Collapse, Temporal Distortion, and Void Echoes are registered awaiting content. Of the six Void Network traits, three are mechanically live — healing, damage amplification, and incoming-damage reduction (Reality Stability), all corruption-scaled; Teleport Access and Shield Recovery are registered. Of the seven Reality Distortion actions, none are mechanically live yet (they operate on the arena/projectiles/pathing rather than the swarm itself, a larger surface than this module's scope) — all seven are registered future content. Shadow Hunter gives AF-033's dormant `teleport` movement behaviour its first EnemyDef producer (a boss already used it); Corruption Parasite, Rift Guardian, and Ancient Void Avatar give the dormant `split`, `merge`, and `cloak` special-ability kinds their first producers. A dev-only spawn key ("6", DEV-guarded beside the debug overlay, alongside the Outlaws' "8", Machines' "9", and Crystals' "7") makes swarm encounters deterministically verifiable in the browser.
