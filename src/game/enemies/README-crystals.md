# crystals — Crystal Ascendancy Enemy Faction (AF-048, game layer, inside `enemies/`)

**Purpose:** The third enemy faction — growth, resonance, cooperation. The deliberate third doctrine alongside AF-046's Outlaws (scatter, fear) and AF-047's Machines (degrade, logic): a Crystal ecosystem WEAKENS CONTINUOUSLY — no state machine at all, just a resonance strength proportional to how many Resonance Nodes remain alive. Destroying a node measurably and immediately weakens every trait the network shares; destroying anything else does not.

**Responsibilities:** Unit/combat-style/special-mechanic/resonance-trait/environmental-control/mini-boss/elite-gain vocabulary, six full sandbox `EnemyDef`s with real AF-032 `WeaponDef` ranged attacks, visual-language record, and resonance/growth tuning (`crystalData`); continuous resonance strength (hard-capped for fairness), the growth-zone factory (`createCrystalGrowth`), and the pure growth-step function (`growCrystalZone`) (`CrystalResonance`).

**Dependencies:** `enemies/enemyData` (`EnemyDef` — unchanged), `bosses/BossArena` (`HazardZoneDef`/`stepHazardZone`/`isInsideHazard` — Crystal Growth's exact engine, unchanged; `growCrystalZone` is the engine's first mechanical extension, not a second engine). Ecosystems reuse AF-046's pure `OutlawSquadRuntime.formationOffsets` for spawn placement (cross-module reuse, not reimplementation). Crystal Titans ARE AF-034 Elites via the unchanged spawn path. The faction is AF-039's *already-profiled* Crystal Dominion — zero new lore. The doctrine Codex entry (AF-043) unlocks on the first Resonance Node kill.

**Data structures:** `CrystalResonanceRuntime`, `ResonanceSnapshot`, `RESONANCE_TUNING`, `CRYSTAL_GROWTH_TUNING`.

**Extension points:** new units are `EnemyDef` data (seven of thirteen registered kinds await defs); new ecosystem compositions are id lists; mini-boss kinds bind to AF-035 `BossDef` content when authored; further Resonance Shared Traits (`RESONANCE_SHARED_TRAITS`) and Environmental Control actions (`ENVIRONMENTAL_CONTROL_ACTIONS`) attach to the same continuous-strength/hazard-engine surfaces.

**Known limitations:** of the ten Special Mechanics, Crystal Growth is mechanically live (via Growth Seeder + the hazard engine); Energy Resonance, Healing Fields, Reflective Crystals, Chain Resonance, Crystal Armour, Terrain Expansion, Living Structures, Prismatic Shields, and Crystal Networks are registered awaiting content. Of the six Resonance Shared Traits, three are mechanically live — healing, outgoing damage bonus, and movement speed, all scaling continuously with strength; Shield Strength, Status Resistance, and Ability Cooldowns are registered. Of the seven Environmental Control actions, only Create Hazards is live; the other six are registered. Crystal "formation"-behaviour units deliberately do not anchor on a command unit the way Outlaw/Machine formation units do — the ecosystem has no single coordinating unit, so they fall back to AF-033's own player-anchored formation default, reinforcing the no-hierarchy design rather than a missing feature. A dev-only spawn key ("7", DEV-guarded beside the debug overlay, alongside the Outlaws' "8" and Machines' "9") makes ecosystem encounters deterministically verifiable in the browser.
