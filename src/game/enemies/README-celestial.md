# celestial — Celestial Conclave Enemy Faction (AF-054, game layer, inside `enemies/`)

**Purpose:** The ninth enemy faction, and the first whose bonus is local rather than global. Every prior doctrine computes ONE shared value or flag for the whole squad (a timer, service flags, a recomputed count, a decaying level, an escalating ladder, a monotonic ratchet, an earned economy, a threshold event). A Constellation is instead a graph of specific pairwise links — not a hub-and-spoke network — and each living entity's bonus is computed from its OWN surviving link count. Two members can carry entirely different bonus magnitudes at the same instant. "Destroying anchor entities destabilises nearby formations" needs no special-cased anchor flag: an anchor is simply whichever unit the graph gives the highest degree, so killing it costs every neighbour a link at once — a cascade that emerges from the graph shape itself.

**Responsibilities:** Unit/combat-style/special-mechanic/network-trait/cosmic-manipulation/mini-boss/elite-gain vocabulary, six full sandbox `EnemyDef`s with real AF-032 `WeaponDef` ranged attacks, visual-language record, and constellation/gravity-well tuning (`celestialData`); the fixed-pattern adjacency graph, per-entity link-count queries, and the three per-entity-scaled Celestial Network bonuses (`CelestialConstellation`).

**Dependencies:** `enemies/enemyData` (`EnemyDef` — unchanged). `bosses/BossArena` (`HazardZoneDef` — Gravity Wells' engine, a sixth reuse after AF-046's mines, AF-048's Crystal Growth, AF-049's Corruption Zones, AF-051's Acid Pools, and AF-053's Singularity Charges). A Living Supernova IS an AF-034 Elite via the unchanged spawn path. **Deliberately does not reuse AF-039's faction-profile system** — the Conclave are ancient cosmic consciousness, not a civilisation, the fourth faction (after AF-049/051/053) to skip a `FactionDef`; its Codex entry cross-references the Paragon Protocol's.

**Data structures:** `CelestialConstellationRuntime`, `ConstellationSnapshot`, `ConstellationMemberSnapshot`, `CONSTELLATION_TUNING`, `GRAVITY_WELL_TUNING`.

**Extension points:** new entities are `EnemyDef` data (seven of thirteen registered kinds await defs); new constellation patterns are edge lists (any graph shape, not just the sandbox's star pattern); mini-boss kinds bind to AF-035 `BossDef` content when authored; further Celestial Network traits (`CELESTIAL_NETWORK_TRAITS`) and Cosmic Manipulation actions (`COSMIC_MANIPULATION_ACTIONS`) attach to the same per-entity link-count surface.

**Known limitations:** of the ten Special Mechanics, Gravity Wells are mechanically live via the Gravity Oracle's `createHazards` ability and the reused hazard engine; Solar Flares, Photon Beams (beyond the Pulsar Hunter's own weapon), Orbital Constructs, Plasma Storms, Constellation Networks (as a system beyond the runtime's own graph), Light Bridges, Solar Winds, and Energy Collapse are registered awaiting content. Of the six Celestial Network traits, three are mechanically live — Solar Energy (damage bonus), Shield Strength (incoming-damage reduction), and Healing/Ability Synchronisation (heal-per-second) — all computed per-entity from `linkCountFor(droneId)`, never from one value shared by the whole formation; Orbital Awareness and Constellation Links (as a shared trait beyond the graph itself) are registered. AF-021's dormant `shieldBreak` status gains its first producer via the Pulsar Hunter's Pulsar Beam. A dev-only spawn key ("1", DEV-guarded beside the debug overlay, alongside the Outlaws' "8", Machines' "9", Crystals' "7", Void's "6", Ancient Custodians' "5", the Xenomorph Hive's "4", the Stellar Nomads' "3", and the Paragon Protocol's "2") makes constellation encounters deterministically verifiable in the browser.
