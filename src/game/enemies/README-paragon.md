# paragon — Paragon Protocol Enemy Faction (AF-053, game layer, inside `enemies/`)

**Purpose:** The eighth enemy faction, and the first driven by a single irreversible threshold event rather than a smooth curve. Every prior doctrine is continuous — a timer, discrete flags, a recomputed count, a decaying level, an escalating-and-capped ladder, a monotonic ratchet, an earned-and-spent economy. Reactor Stability depletes from incoming damage and its own inherent instability, and the instant it hits zero, Containment Collapse fires exactly once — permanently inverting the unit from defended-but-fragile (Adaptive Shields) to unshielded-but-far-more-dangerous (Energy Overload + Unstable Reactors). Nothing in the roster reverses direction like this.

**Responsibilities:** Unit/combat-style/special-mechanic/adaptive-technology/mini-boss/elite-gain vocabulary, six full sandbox `EnemyDef`s with real AF-032 `WeaponDef` ranged attacks, visual-language record, and instability/singularity tuning (`paragonData`); the depleting Stability meter, the Containment Sentinel's active repair, the one-shot collapse-event flag, and the shields-then-overload inversion (`ParagonInstability`).

**Dependencies:** `enemies/enemyData` (`EnemyDef` — unchanged). `bosses/BossArena` (`HazardZoneDef` — Singularity Charges' engine, a fifth reuse after AF-046's mines, AF-048's Crystal Growth, AF-049's Corruption Zones, and AF-051's Acid Pools). An Omega Prototype IS an AF-034 Elite via the unchanged spawn path. **Deliberately does not reuse AF-039's faction-profile system** — the Paragon Protocol is abandoned pre-Collapse military research belonging to no single civilisation, the same non-civilisation framing AF-049's Void Swarm and AF-051's Xenomorph Hive used; its Codex entry cross-references the Xenomorph Hive's.

**Data structures:** `ParagonInstabilityRuntime`, `InstabilitySnapshot`, `PARAGON_INSTABILITY_TUNING`, `SINGULARITY_CHARGE_TUNING`.

**Extension points:** new prototypes are `EnemyDef` data (seven of thirteen registered kinds await defs); new protocol compositions are id lists; mini-boss kinds bind to AF-035 `BossDef` content when authored; further Adaptive Technology inputs (`ADAPTIVE_TECHNOLOGY_INPUTS`) attach to the same `recordIncomingDamage`-style analysis surface AF-047's Adaptive AI first established.

**Known limitations:** of the ten Special Mechanics, Containment Collapse and Energy Overload are the doctrine's mechanically-live core; Adaptive Shields is live pre-collapse; Unstable Reactors is live post-collapse; Singularity Charges' hazard shape is defined (`SINGULARITY_CHARGE_TUNING`) but its main-loop seeding is the composition root's job, wired alongside the collapse event; Quantum Shift is live as the Prototype Drone's `teleport` movement; Experimental Ammunition, Dimensional Pulse, Prototype Drones (as a spawned-reinforcement mechanic, distinct from the unit kind of the same name), and Energy Fractures are registered awaiting content. Of the seven Adaptive Technology inputs, only Incoming Damage is mechanically live (`recordIncomingDamage`, the same pattern AF-047's Adaptive AI established for `damageTypes`); the other six are registered. AF-021's dormant `overload` status and AF-032's dormant `gravityAffected` projectile behaviour both gain their first producers here. A dev-only spawn key ("2", DEV-guarded beside the debug overlay, alongside the Outlaws' "8", Machines' "9", Crystals' "7", Void's "6", Ancient Custodians' "5", the Xenomorph Hive's "4", and the Stellar Nomads' "3") makes protocol encounters — including a forced Containment Collapse — deterministically verifiable in the browser.
